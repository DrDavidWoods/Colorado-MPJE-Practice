# Coverage expansion — version 6

200 active questions: 120 retained and 80 new. Compounding now has 64 questions (60 new and 4 existing).

## Learning sequence

1. In Topic Practice, choose Foundations and Compounding. Read the post-answer explanation, rule summary, and individual distractor rationales.
2. Repeat practice to reach unseen questions; each set has at most 30 questions.
3. Switch to Application for cases involving deadlines, categories, ingredient limits, and competing requirements.
4. Practice Emergency Kits and Pharmacy Operations, then mix topics and use the timed simulation.

Foundations uses editorial difficulty levels 1–2; Application uses 3–5. These are study filters, not validated measurements of exam difficulty. Authority and explanations remain hidden until answering; exam feedback remains deferred.

## Source scope and limitations

New items were checked against the linked Colorado Board rules PDF (3 CCR 719-1, version 11936), FDA compounding guidance pages, and 21 CFR 1304.11 on September 19, 2026. Each item includes the applicable rule or section and a primary-source link. The source-check date is not a legal effective date or certification that no later amendment exists. The original 120 items retain their earlier verification dates.

This expansion develops foundations; it does not establish complete coverage of every MPJE competency or replace reading current governing authority. Remaining areas for deeper development include bulk-substance eligibility and 503A anticipatory limits, additional sterile monitoring and remediation details, radiopharmaceutical compounding, full hazardous-drug lifecycle controls, specialized institutional services, and broader federal acquisition/disposal requirements. No official or recalled examination items are used. No psychometric validation or comprehensive independent legal audit is claimed.

The practice-bank distribution intentionally gives extra space to compounding. Simulation still selects 120 questions using the existing area quotas (26/40/29/25); it is not an adaptive NABP exam.
