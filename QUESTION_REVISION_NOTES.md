# Question revision — v5.0.0

## Scope and limits
Reviewed the 120-item v4 bank for obvious construction clues. Substantively revised 78 stems and/or answer sets. This is an editorial improvement, not an independently calibrated exam or a full new legal audit. Some basic recall questions remain intentionally straightforward for a learner who knows the rule. Original authority dates are retained; the whole bank is not relabeled as newly legally verified.

## Changes
- Replaced omission-and-restatement questions with required record fields, responsible actors, deadlines, scope boundaries, and operational choices.
- Replaced several implausible distractors with related requirements, competing time periods, and alternative authorization pathways.
- Replaced an ambiguous Schedule II partial-fill item with explicit written and first-fill dates.
- Corrected a manager-change timing item whose original dates could make both deadlines late; clarified manager-change paperwork versus ownership changes.
- Corrected the license-transfer experience item to reflect the one-year licensure OR qualifying original-internship alternative.
- Replaced the compromised-container common-sense question with a specific Colorado sterile-product recall obligation, citing the governing rule rather than an exam blueprint.
- Hid authority access until practice submission, with a matching handler guard; exam reference access stays unavailable during the attempt.
- Hid subjective difficulty labels during answering. Existing difficulty filters remain author estimates, not validated exam difficulty.
- Updated cache and asset versions to 5.0.0. Scores/history are retained; prior scores reflect prior wording.

## Source checks
Targeted checks used the Colorado Board rules, including confidentiality, order documentation, collaborative practice, vaccination records, delivery/storage, opening hours and sterile-product recalls; HB26-1336 was consulted for the revised statutory-scope questions. Existing source links remain in post-answer feedback. Federal source access was limited; unchanged federal legal propositions retain their earlier source baseline. This is not a fresh certification of all 120 answer keys.

- Colorado Board rules: https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?fileName=3+CCR+719-1&ruleVersionId=11936
- HB26-1336: https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/HB26-1336
- DEA prescription guidance: https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/faq/prescriptions-faq.html

## Validation performed
JavaScript syntax checks for app, bank and service worker; 120 active unique question IDs; distinct option IDs/text within each question; answer keys resolve to options; one correct key for non-multiple-response items; explanation and authority coverage; all four area counts retained (26/40/29/25). No device/browser visual test performed for this wording-focused update. Historical QA reports are marked as historical and do not certify this release.
